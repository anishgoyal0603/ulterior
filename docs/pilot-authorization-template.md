# Pilot audit — written permission (template)

> Use this before Ulterior audits any business's website on the server side.
> Fill the blanks, send it to the business owner, and start only after you have
> it back signed (a signed PDF or a reply email that quotes it in full is fine).
> This is a practical template, not legal advice; have it read by someone
> qualified before you rely on it commercially.

### 1. What we are asking permission to do

The Business permits team The Odyssey to run Ulterior, an automated tool,
against the website(s) below, to look for design patterns that resemble those
described in India's *Guidelines for Prevention and Regulation of Dark Patterns,
2023*, and to give the Business a written report.

### 2. When

____:____ and ____:____ IST unless agreed otherwise.

### 3. How the tool behaves

- It opens pages in a browser the way a shopper does, at a gentle pace (no load
  testing, no attempts to break security).
- It **never places an order, pays, subscribes or signs in as anyone**. It refuses
  to click buttons such as Pay, Place Order, Subscribe and Sign in.
- If the Business wants pages behind a login reviewed, the Business provides a
  **test account** for that purpose: ____________________ (leave blank if none).
  No real customer account is used.
- Requests come from: ____________________ (server / IP, if the Business wants to
  allow-list it).

### 4. What happens to the results

- The report goes **only to the Business**. Team The Odyssey will not publish the
  Business's name, findings or screenshots without the Business's separate written
  consent.
- Findings describe characteristics that *resemble* patterns in the Guidelines.
  They are observations, **not** a legal determination that the Business has
  breached any law.
- Text captured from pages is redacted of personal details before storage.
  Audit data is deleted within ____ days of the report (30 by default), or sooner
  on request.
- Team The Odyssey may describe the pilot in anonymised form (for example, "a
  Bhopal fashion store reduced checkout fees shown late from 3 to 0") **only if
  the Business ticks here:** [ ] Yes [ ] No

### 5. Either side can stop

The Business can withdraw this permission at any time by email to the contact
above; audits stop the same day.
