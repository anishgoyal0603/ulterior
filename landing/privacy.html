Privacy

What Ulterior reads, keeps and sends

Last updated 12 October 2026 · applies to the Ulterior browser extension (version 2.2 and later) and this website.

The short version. The extension works inside your browser. What you browse is not sent to us or to anyone else — no page content, no browsing history, no analytics, no advertising identifiers. Two features can send something, and both are off unless you switch them on (your browser asks your permission first): a report of a page that you review and send yourself, and community prices, which share the prices of products you open (product id and price only) to build price history for everyone.

1. The browser extension

What it reads

On the shopping, food and travel sites listed in the extension (for example Amazon.in, Flipkart, BookMyShow, MakeMyTrip), it reads the page you are looking at: prices, fee lines, totals, the text of buttons and checkboxes, and whether options were already selected. On any other website it reads nothing unless you press Scan this page, and then only that one page.

It never reads password fields and never asks for your password. It runs inside your own browser session, so it sees what you see and nothing more.

What it keeps, and where

  • Results for open tabs, the price of the option you picked, countdown readings and pop-ups you closed — held in your browser's memory (storage.session). They disappear when you close the browser, and a page from one website can never read another website's.
  • Your price history (optional, on by default) — for product pages you open: a product identifier such as amazon.in:B0XXXXXXXX, the price, the date, any “only N left” number the page showed, and a scrambled fingerprint of the product name (each word turned into a short code) so the same product can be recognised on another shop for price comparison. No product name, no page address, nothing about you. Stored only on your device (storage.local), limited to 400 products and 180 days. Turn it off or clear it from the extension's popup.
  • Your watchlist — products you chose to watch: the product's name and link as you saw them (the link without anything after a “?”), your target price, the prices seen, and the price alerts you were shown. Up to 100 products, on your device only; remove any of them on the watchlist page.
  • Your settings (on-page alerts, price check, price history, community prices and reporting on or off).

Text quoted in a finding is passed through a redaction step that removes email addresses, phone numbers, card-like numbers, PAN numbers and booking references before it is kept.

What it sends

The checks make no network requests. Copy evidence and Copy diagnostics put plain text on your clipboard for you to paste wherever you choose; they name the website but deliberately leave out the page address. Compare prices links are ordinary links that open a store's search in a new tab only when you click one.

Community prices (optional). If you switch on Community prices in the popup, your browser first asks your permission to contact our server. After that, when you open a product page on a listed shop, the extension sends that product's id (for example amazon.in:B0XXXXXXXX) and the price shown — nothing else: no page address, no product name, no account, no cookies. In return it fetches the community's daily prices for that product to draw a longer history, and every six hours it asks for today's community price of the products on your watchlist, so it can tell you about a drop. Our server stores the product id, the price and the date only — not your address or anything that identifies you — and serves each day's price as the median of everyone's reports.

Reports you choose to send. If you switch on Let me send reports to Ulterior in the popup, your browser first asks your permission to contact our server. After that, a Report this page button appears when something was flagged. It shows you the exact report — the website's name, the page type (product, checkout or other), the extension version, the time, and the findings with their evidence, already redacted — and sends it only when you press Send. It never includes the page address, cookies, your account or anything that identifies you. Switch reporting off at any time; the browser permission is removed with it.

Why it asks for each permission

  • Read and change data on the listed shopping and travel sites — to run its checks there automatically. “Change” is used only to show the small Ulterior alert in the corner of the page.
  • activeTab and scripting — so Scan this page can check a site that is not on the list, once, when you ask.
  • storage — for the items listed above.
  • alarms and notifications — to check your watchlist every six hours (only with community prices on) and to tell you when a watched product reaches your target.
  • Access to ulterior-api-production.up.railway.app (optional, asked for only when you switch reporting or community prices on) — for those two features and nothing else.

Removing everything

Uninstalling the extension deletes everything it stored. Clear price history in the popup deletes the history at any time.

2. This website and the audit service

The live demo audits two practice storefronts that are part of this website. Its results are stored in our database like any other audit.

The audit dashboard is for people we have given an access key. Audits run there visit the website addresses entered, record what the pages showed, and store the findings with screenshots. Text taken from audited pages is redacted before storage. Audits are deleted automatically after the retention period set on the server (30 days by default), and only a limited number are kept.

Community prices are stored as product id, price and date, kept for about 13 months, and served as daily medians. Nothing about who sent them is stored.

Reports sent from the extension are stored with the website name, page type, version and findings only — not the sender's address or any identifier. They are read by the team, deleted after the same retention period as audits, and only a limited number are kept.

The website sets no cookies and uses no analytics or advertising scripts. The server's access log records requests (time, address, page) for operating and securing the service.

3. What Ulterior's findings are

Findings are automated observations that a page shows characteristics resembling patterns defined in India's Guidelines for Prevention and Regulation of Dark Patterns, 2023. They are not a legal determination about any business.
