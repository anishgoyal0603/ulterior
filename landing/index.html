How it works

From a link to a verdict in four steps

  1. 1

    Walk

    A real Chromium opens the shop and clicks through listing, product, cart and checkout. It will never press Pay, Place order, Subscribe or Sign in.

  2. 2

    Detect

    Four detectors read what the shopper saw: prices and page state, the journey across pages, on-screen size and contrast, and the wording.

  3. 3

    Score

    Each finding gets an evidence tier with a hard ceiling — 100%, 90% or 60%. No detector is allowed to claim more than its evidence can carry.

  4. 4

    Report

    A PDF report, a live dashboard and a browser extension. A page it could not read is reported as inconclusive — never as clean.

Evidence, not a label

Every finding comes with receipts

A label on its own can't be checked. So Ulterior keeps what the page actually showed, and anyone can reopen it and see for themselves.

  • The page's own words, quoted exactly
  • The step it happened on — listing, cart or checkout
  • The numbers behind it, not just a score
  • A screenshot of the page in the PDF report
