How to read a finding

Provable — arithmetic or DOM state. The page either did this or it didn't. Capped at 100% confidence.
Corroborated — two or more independent signals had to agree. Capped at 90%.
Indicative — consistent with the pattern; a human should confirm it. Capped at 60%.

No detector may report more confidence than its tier allows.
